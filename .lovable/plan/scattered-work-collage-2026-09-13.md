# Scattered Work Collage

## Goal
Keep the existing photographed paper exactly as it is and transform only the Selected Work arrangement into a loose, asymmetric editorial collage.

## Changes
- Make the entire visible work area one uninterrupted physical paper sheet, with no generic website background framing or interrupting it.
- Place every project image independently at a deliberately different horizontal and vertical position, scale, and distance from the paper edges.
- Avoid shared alignment lines, equal distribution, repeated spacing, columns, rows, masonry, and any other visible grid logic.
- Let the paper folds remain incidental photographed details; do not align images or captions to them and do not use them to define sections.
- Place each caption independently near its corresponding image, with varied proximity and surrounding space while keeping the association clear.
- Use restrained, natural overlaps only where they reinforce the feeling of physical prints laid onto a sheet; never obscure imagery or text.
- Preserve all project content, imagery, typography, colours, paper texture, folds, imperfections, navigation, and surrounding sections.
- On mobile, retain the same art-directed irregularity through varied widths and alternating offsets while maintaining a clear reading order.

## Validation
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for deliberately unaligned placement, clear image-caption associations, readable text, continuous paper coverage, and no clipped content.
- Confirm the result does not read as a grid, masonry layout, evenly distributed collage, or fold-aligned composition.
- Confirm all four projects render and the site builds without errors.

## Technical details
- Update only the Selected Work spatial positioning rules and the minimum markup needed to support independent placement.
- Use freeform absolute positioning on desktop rather than grid or masonry layout primitives.
- Do not modify or regenerate the photographed paper asset.
