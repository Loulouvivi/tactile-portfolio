Keep the existing photographed paper exactly as it is and transform only the Selected Work arrangement into a loose, asymmetric editorial collage.

- Preserve all project content, imagery, typography, colours, paper texture, folds, imperfections, navigation, and surrounding sections.
- Keep the mobile version readable as a loose single-column composition without cards or boxed panels.

## Validation
