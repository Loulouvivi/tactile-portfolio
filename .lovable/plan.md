# Continuous Homepage Paper Sheet

## Goal
Remove the visual seam between the opening composition and Selected Work so they read as consecutive arrangements on one uninterrupted sheet of the existing photographed folded paper.

## Changes
- Place the existing folded-paper photograph once behind a shared wrapper containing both the hero and Selected Work.
- Remove the separate hero and Selected Work paper images without changing their foreground content or composition.
- Remove the spacing and clipped paper-board treatment that creates a white band or detached second sheet at the transition.
- Keep the current hero positions, Selected Work scatter, typography, imagery, navigation, motion, and all later page sections unchanged.
- Verify the join at desktop and mobile widths, including the `#work` anchor.
